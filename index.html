Many goods. One building.

MixedStorage lets a single warehouse or pile hold several kinds of goods, and you choose how much space each one gets, by percentage.

What it does

Divide a building's capacity, good by good

A 1,200-capacity warehouse can reserve 50% for carrots (600) and 50% for gears (600). Percentages reserve space. They don't create goods or instantly fill the building, so your beavers keep hauling by the normal rules.

Mix goods by percentage

Give any good the building normally accepts a share of its capacity, down to 0.01%. Shares must total exactly 100%, so every slot has a purpose.

Stock at a glance

Summary cards show each good's icon, stored / limit count, allocated percentage, and a fill bar, including incoming deliveries and excess stock.

Quick setup

Search the goods list, show only allocated goods, click Max to give one good everything, or copy an allocation and paste it into another compatible building. The game's own Duplicate settings tool copies allocations too. Edits stay drafts until you Apply.

Safe with your stock

Lowering a limit never deletes goods. Extras are flagged as excess and leave the normal way, and deliveries already on their way are counted before a change is accepted.

Visible in the world

Warehouses and piles show the mix using the game's own goods models, divided by allocation. The panel always has the exact numbers.

Multiplayer-ready

BeaverBuddies support is built in and switches on automatically when BeaverBuddies is installed. Applied allocations synchronize between players.

Looks native

Uses the game's own panel frames, buttons, input fields, checkbox, and scrollbar.

Controls stay in reach

The goods list scrolls while Copy, Paste, the total, and Apply / Revert stay in a fixed footer.

Fits your screen

The editor is sized to your window and leaves room for the game's Construction site panel.

Things to try

  1. Every good a Folktails warehouse accepts is in the list. Use Search or scroll, set Bread to 25 and Wheat flour to 75, then press Apply 100%. Bread is now over its new limit. Lowering a limit never deletes stock, so the extra shows as excess.
  2. Type 60 next to 50. The total turns red and Apply stays unavailable until it's exactly 100%.
  3. Load A tiny share to see the panel warn that a good rounds to 0 items.
  4. Press Copy allocations, switch to the other building, then Paste. The percentages carry over and the limits are recalculated for the new capacity.

In the world

Watch the mix take shape

Warehouses and piles show what's inside using the game's own goods models and materials, divided by allocation. The visual split is approximate, since whole visible cells can't always match a percentage exactly. The panel always shows the exact counts.

Banners still show a single representative good, and if a model can't be divided, MixedStorage falls back to the original visuals.

Get started

Up and running in three steps

  1. Download and extract

    Grab the ZIP from the release's Assets and extract its MixedStorage folder into Documents\Timberborn\Mods.

  2. Enable it

    Turn on Harmony and MixedStorage in the game's mod manager and restart when prompted.

  3. Mix your storage

    Select a supported warehouse or pile, enter percentages that total 100% in Storage Allocation, and click Apply 100%.

Project status

MixedStorage 1.0.0 is the first official release. Its panel layout and storage visuals are unchanged from v0.5.8, which was checked in game. The 1.0.0 fixes (the game's Duplicate settings tool, clearer error messages, and loading damaged saves) haven't been played in game yet. Players have reported successful use of earlier builds, and automated checks cover the allocation math, mesh geometry, native game API calls, and optional multiplayer loading. Those checks don't replace live gameplay testing, particularly for multiplayer.

Found something odd? Check the troubleshooting guide or open an issue.
